Source and caveats
SOURCE RECORD / S-BIAD1057

What you’re looking at.

Fixed fluorescence from a mouse blastomere compaction study. The opening image projects 14 sampled planes from one 61-plane TIFF. Scrolling separates seven of those same measured planes. The scan line and the movement between views are animation. The specimen is fixed, not a live recording, and none of the pixels were generated.

Measurement

The CSV holds per-plane, per-channel means and 99th percentiles from the original 16-bit pixels. Color, contrast and crop are display settings, not stain assignments. Plane positions are ordinal, not calibrated depths.

Field note · GFP got its start in a jellyfish

Green fluorescent protein was first isolated from the jellyfish Aequorea victoria. Under blue light, it fluoresces green. The colors in this viewer are for display; green here doesn’t identify GFP. Nobel Prize source
