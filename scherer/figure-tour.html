Deep dive · figure-by-figure walkthrough

A figure-by-figure trip through the Rosi-GIPR analysis

Nine acts, in the order the evidence actually builds — not the order the files happen to be numbered. Every description below was written from direct review of the figure itself; nothing is reconstructed from filenames alone.

The short version going in: genotype (GIPR overexpression) reshapes this tissue far more than any drug does. Rosiglitazone's own effect is real, but roughly 5× smaller, and GIPR overexpression blunts most of it. The 53-gene Tier A list in Act 8 is what survives every robustness check as genuinely genotype-specific.

01

Setup & QC — are these 12 samples trustworthy?

Before any comparison means anything, the libraries need to be clean and the pipeline needs to be legible.

Fig. 01

Library QC and PCA

Library sizes sit in a healthy 18–36M range for all 12 pools — no sample needs to be dropped. The PCA is the whole study in one plot: PC1 (78% of variance) splits WT from GIPR-Adip with zero overlap — this single axis is why the baseline genotype contrast returns 10,379 genes. PC2 (14.3%) further separates WT-vehicle from WT-Rosi, while GA pools stay clustered together regardless of treatment — an early visual preview that Rosi moves WT fat more than it moves GA fat. A third panel flags Myh4 spiking hard in two of three WT-Rosi pools specifically, not uniformly — the first sighting of the muscle-signature caveat that recurs through Acts 4–5.

Fig. 02

The pipeline, end to end

Five input sources (the 12 pooled libraries, a published GIPR-Adip baseline, a 3T3-L1 PPAR-γ reference, a myeloid single-cell reference, and a composition atlas) feed a core DESeq2 ~0 + group model, which branches into sensitivity refits (muscle covariate, voom weights, 22 composition variants, leave-one-pool-out) and external benchmarks (published concordance, PPAR-γ benchmark, myeloid-state projection) — all converging on the figures, three Excel workbooks, and the report. This is the map for every act that follows.

02

Genotype dominates — before any drug is given

GIPR overexpression alone reshapes this tissue on a scale that makes everything else look small by comparison.

Archive 01 — Contrast C1

GIPR-Adip vs WT, vehicle: 10,379 genes

A dense, symmetric double-tornado — both wings pinned at the plot's significance ceiling, 5,109 genes up and 5,270 down in GIPR-Adip fat. Spp1, Il1rn, Cd14, Pirb lead the up side; Klf14, Folh1, Igfals, Phgdh lead the down side. This is the loudest, most saturated signal anywhere in the study.

Archive 04 — Contrast C4

Same comparison, on drug: 10,493 genes

Visually almost indistinguishable from C1 — same saturated shape, same scale. 81% of these genes are also significant in C1. The drug barely moves this picture.

Archive 06 — All four, side by side

The whole study in one glance

C1 and C4 (top-left, bottom-right) are both huge and near-identical. C2 and C3 (top-right, bottom-left) are an order of magnitude smaller, and C3 is the smallest of all four. One image, the whole finding.

Fig. 03

C1 next to the interaction term — scale check

Placed side by side with the actual question this study asks (the interaction, right panel): C1's cloud dwarfs it. The interaction panel caps at -log10(padj) ≈ 15 versus C1's ≈ 100, and spans a narrower fold-change range. Myh4, Actn3, Mylk2, Atp2a1 — the recurring muscle cluster — sit on its down side at modest significance; Tsku, Aldh1a1, Serpina3n, Mrc2, Efemp1 anchor the up side, a plausible ECM/remodeling signature.

Fig. 04

Why genotype hits so hard: this isn't the same tissue

Deconvolution shows WT pools are adipocyte-dominant (60–80% estimated mRNA share) regardless of treatment, while every single GA pool — vehicle or Rosi — flips to myeloid-dominant (75–85%). Part of what C1/C4 are detecting is a real compositional shift in the tissue itself, which is exactly why the sensitivity models in Act 6 exist.

03

What the drug does — and why it's lopsided

Rosiglitazone's canonical PPAR-γ program fires in both genotypes. It just fires much harder in one of them.

Archive 02 — C2

Rosi in WT fat: 2,084 genes

A narrow "V" peaking around -log10(padj)≈28 (Mbnl3) — real, but nowhere near C1's scale. A muscle-marker cluster (Tnni2, Myh4, Actn3) sits at large fold-change but modest significance on the far right.

Archive 03 — C3

Same drug, in GA fat: 747 genes

The sparsest of the four primary contrasts — clear gaps between labeled genes, narrower fold-change range. A third of C2's hit count. GIPR overexpression is visibly blunting the response to the same drug.

Fig. 05

Is this really PPAR-γ biology? Yes, in both genotypes.

Canonical Rosi/PPAR-γ targets (Elovl3, Acaa1b, Ephx2, Pdk4) track the identity line when compared against an independent 3T3-L1 Rosi signature — in both WT and GA fat. Enrichment scores mostly confirm it in C2, where Rosi-down genes are significantly suppressed and Rosi-up genes significantly induced. In C3, the Rosi-up set still tracks the same direction, but the Rosi-down set is actually significantly enriched upward (NES +1.50, padj 0.012) — the opposite sign from C2. The drug's target genes are the same; what differs is how far each genotype lets it go, and in GA fat one arm of the program doesn't just weaken, it partially inverts.

Fig. 09

Pathway-level view: the asymmetry, quantified

In WT fat, oxidative phosphorylation is the single loudest bar in the entire figure (NES 3.1) — a genuine mitochondrial induction. In GA fat, the same pathways move in the same direction but at a fraction of the strength. The interaction panel is almost uniformly blue: GIPR overexpression dampens essentially every one of the drug's pathway effects, not just a select few.

Fig. 06

Does this story survive every model variant? Yes.

C1 and C4 show the same "adipocyte identity traded for inflammation/proliferation" signature whether or not a muscle covariate is added. The interaction columns consistently light up growth-factor and cell-cycle gene sets across every sensitivity variant (INT, INTnoMus, INTmusT, INTadj) — the pathway-level finding isn't an artifact of one particular model choice.

04

The thermogenic program — switched on in WT, stalled in GA

Fat-burning and fat-making machinery both respond to Rosi — in wild-type fat. In GIPR-Adip fat, they largely stay off.

Fig. 18

The single clearest summary image in the whole report

WT-Rosi's polygon balloons outward on fatty-acid oxidation, lipolysis, lipogenesis, PPAR-γ targets, and thermogenic core — dwarfing the other three groups on every axis but one. GA-vehicle and GA-Rosi sit almost on top of each other, collapsed near the center, bulging out only on creatine cycling. On calcium cycling (SERCA/RyR) it's the opposite of a bulge — both GA groups sit at the lowest of all four groups on that axis, underscoring rather than resolving the open Ryr2 caveat from Act 5. If you only look at one figure from this study, this is the one.

Fig. 17

Gene-by-gene, pool-by-pool

A block of intense red confined almost entirely to the WT-Rosi columns across fatty-acid oxidation, lipolysis, and lipogenesis — pale or blue in GA-Rosi for the identical rows. The C2 column is dense with significance stars; C3 is comparatively quiet.

Fig. 19

Naming names

Acaa1b, Slc27a1, Ehhadh, Gk, Acox1 carry robust triple-asterisk induction by Rosi in WT; Fabp3 (**) and Acadm (*) are induced too, just at slightly lower significance. The same seven genes are mostly non-significant in GA, and a few (Scd2, Pparg) even trend the wrong way. This is the gene list behind Fig. 17/18.

Fig. 10

The TCA cycle itself follows the same rule

WT-Rosi pools are saturated red across nearly the whole TCA/PDH gene block; GA-Rosi shows the same rows muted and mixed. GA-vehicle columns actually skew down relative to WT-vehicle for several TCA/PDH genes at baseline — Pdha1, Dlat, Idh2, Idh3a, Mpc1/2 all significantly lower in GA before any drug is even given — the exception is Pdk1, which runs significantly higher in GA at baseline, the opposite direction from the rest of the complex.

Archive 07 — KEGG mmu03320

The PPAR signaling map itself

Fatty-acid oxidation/transport genes (FABP1, ACS, CPT-1) and the thermogenesis gene UCP-1 read red (up); PPARγ/RXR itself and several lipogenesis genes read mixed — a nuanced remodeling, not a simple on/off switch.

Archive 08 — KEGG mmu04714

The canonical thermogenesis map

UCP1, CPT1, and the lipolysis machinery read red; AMPK and the electron-transport-chain complex read blue, sitting right beside a red ATPase box — a split within the OXPHOS machinery itself, worth a closer look before anyone calls this "classic browning."

05

The part that doesn't fit neatly

A real muscle-like gene signature shows up in some pools. It's genuine adipose biology — but it isn't uniform, and the calcium story it suggests only half holds up.

Fig. 20

Not every WT-Rosi pool carries this signature

WT_1_Rosi and WT_2_Rosi show near-saturated red across the entire muscle gene list (Myh4, Acta1, Ckm, Ryr1...); WT_3_Rosi is pale for the same genes. The "correlation with Myh4" bars (mostly 0.5–0.9) confirm these genes move as one coordinated module — a real, recognized beige-fat/muscle-like program, cited against prior Kajimura-lab work, not a one-off artifact, but one that isn't equally present in every replicate.

Fig. 11

The calcium story only half works

Atp2a2 (SERCA2) is barely different across groups — only a slight baseline bump in GA. Ryr2 is actually lower in GA, both on and off drug — the opposite of a simple "more calcium cycling in GA" narrative. Meanwhile Atp2a1, Myh4, Ckm, Ryr1 (the fast-skeletal module) climb steeply in WT-Rosi specifically, with wide pool-to-pool variance.

Fig. 15

But Atp2a1, Ccl2, and Timp1 do carry a real signal

Atp2a1's interaction term is significant and negative — Rosi induces it specifically in WT, not GA. Ccl2 is already higher in GA at baseline and gets suppressed by Rosi in WT; Timp1 trends the same way in WT but doesn't reach significance there (padj 0.13). Neither gene stays flat in GA, though — both rise significantly with Rosi in GIPR-Adip fat (Ccl2 padj 0.048; Timp1 padj 0.018), a genuine genotype-dependent divergence, just not the clean "suppressed in WT, unchanged in GA" story the muscle signature might suggest at first glance.

06

Does any of this survive scrutiny?

Before trusting the interaction term, it gets stress-tested two ways: drop one pool at a time, and swap the tissue-composition correction.

What this is checking If a finding only shows up with one specific pool included, or only under one specific composition correction, it's fragile — and shouldn't be the headline.
Fig. 13

Drop one pool at a time

C1 and C4 barely move — rock-solid near 10,000 genes no matter which pool is excluded. C3 is the most volatile, swinging from under 300 to ~900. Gdf15's C1 and C3 effects stay tight across every refit; its interaction estimate hovers near zero and never reaches significance in any of the 12 refits (padj ranging 0.52–0.89) — visual confirmation of the padj 0.69 caveat, not a flip.

Fig. 14

Swap the composition correction

Same pattern: C1/C4 barely move across the different adjustment methods tested; C3 and the interaction term are the most sensitive. The named candidate genes — Gdf15, Atp2a2, Ryr2 — sit nearly on top of each other across the correction methods. Itgax and Gpnmb are flagged as the two genes most sensitive to which correction is used.

07

Checked against the published record

The baseline genotype signature gets compared, gene by gene, against an independently published GIPR-Adip dataset.

Fig. 12

13,910 shared genes, r=0.54, 92.6% directional agreement

Thousands of genes form a clear positive diagonal against the published data (PMID 39642881). Fast-skeletal sarcomere genes (Myh4, Ckm, Atp2a1) point the same direction in both datasets — often more strongly here than in the published 2-week data. SERCA/RyR calcium genes point the other way in both — the Ryr2 discrepancy from Act 5 isn't unique to this dataset; it replicates.

08

The actual deliverable: 53 genes that survive everything

Out of 861 genes with a significant genotype × Rosi interaction, only genes that are GIPR-specific and survive both sensitivity refits make the Tier A list — 20 of which are secreted.

Fig. 07

The 53, clustered

One gene block (Igfbp3, Pxdn, Zdhhc2, Qsox1, Acot4...) is high in WT-vehicle, low in GA-vehicle, and reverses specifically under Rosi in GA — the textbook GIPR-specific pattern. A second, larger block (Cyp4f39, Pianp, Mmp12, Ctss, Lyz2 — mostly myeloid/immune genes) runs high in GA regardless of drug, more a baseline mark than an interaction. A smaller ECM/interferon subset (Wnt9a, Lox, Serpinh1, Ccl12, Tnc, Timp1) is most intense specifically in GA-Rosi — the clearest visual signature of "Rosi only does this because GIPR is overexpressed."

Fig. 08

The named candidates, honestly scored

Gipr confirms the model (log2FC ~5, padj 2e-61). Gdf15 is ~26-fold higher in GA at baseline and rises further with Rosi only in GA — but its interaction term is grey/non-significant, the CI visibly crossing zero. Smpd3 is the one gene here with a genuinely significant interaction (padj 2e-4): Rosi raises it in WT but not GA.

Fig. 16

The circulating-factor candidates

Every gene here is titled "Rosi response differs by genotype." F10 is elevated in GA at baseline, then pulled down hard by Rosi only in GA. Col12a1 is flat at baseline (padj 0.98) but rises selectively under Rosi in GA. Wnt9a, by contrast, starts out significantly lower in GA at baseline (padj 2.7e-6) before also rising selectively under Rosi in GA — its trend line crosses twice, not once. This is where "GIPR-specific secreted candidate" stops being a phrase and becomes six visibly crossing trend lines.

Archive 05 — Interaction term

Where the 53 actually come from

Chst11 stands alone at the top. 519 up vs 342 down — a real asymmetry. The muscle cluster reappears faintly on the far left at low significance; Tsku, Aldh1a1, Serpina3n, Mrc2, Efemp1 anchor the upper-right, the same ECM signature that survives into Tier A.

What this section is not saying That GDF15 causes appetite suppression, or that calcium cycling explains the muscle signature. Both remain open hypotheses for follow-up, not findings — the report says so explicitly, and this tour has tried not to smooth that over anywhere above.
09

Who's actually in the tissue

A last check on the composition story from Act 2: which specific immune cell types are driving the "GA fat is myeloid-shifted" signal?

Fig. 21

Lipid-associated macrophages and Ly6c-hi monocytes, specifically

Against an external myeloid reference, genes up in GA at baseline score strongly positive in lipid-associated macrophages (0.9) and proliferating myeloid cells (0.6), and negative in dendritic/lymphoid states. The Rosi-in-GA and interaction-up gene sets both peak hardest in Ly6c-hi monocytes. The composition shift from Act 2 isn't a generic "more immune cells" story — it's specifically toward these two states.
